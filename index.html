What the rates mean

Each row is one qualifying fare for one departure. Click a card to include that tier; click again to remove it. Several cards can be active at once.

Economy

Cabin and meals; extras on board

The cruise fare for a balcony cabin or better, without a drinks or Wi-Fi package. Main dining is included; gratuities, drinks, and excursions are usually paid separately. Celebrity’s own search only publishes this kind of lead-in price, so its rows are always Economy. A sailing is listed here when that fare is under 175 EUR / 200 USD per night.

All included

Drinks and Wi-Fi bundled in

A fare the agency marks as all-inclusive — typically a drinks package and Wi-Fi on top of the same cabin standard. Contitrans uses ALLINC/N1K tariff codes; Crusit spells the fare out as All Inclusive. Holland America’s bundled fares land here too. Listed when the bundle is under 300 EUR / 345 USD per night.

Ultra

Luxury all-inclusive, or a ship-within-a-ship

The whole ship on Seabourn, Silversea, Regent, or Viking; or a premium enclave such as Celebrity The Retreat, NCL The Haven, or MSC Yacht Club. MSC and NCL only appear in this tier — their cheaper cabins are ignored. Listed under 350 EUR / 405 USD per night.

  • Prices are per person, in the currency that website published. Nothing is converted, so a dollar figure is not comparable to a euro one.
  • Per night is the fare divided by the nights the sailing spans (not the marketed day count).
  • Interior cabins are never listed. An ordinary ocean-view stateroom is dropped too, but an ocean-view suite on an all-suite line is kept.
