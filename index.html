Cruise Rates

Affordable premium and ultra fares

Overview

This page is a snapshot of affordable premium and ultra cruise fares collected from Bulgarian travel agencies and from the cruise lines’ own sites. It is not a booking engine: each row links out to the page that published that fare.

Interior cabins and ordinary ocean-view staterooms are already excluded. What remains is a balcony cabin or better, an all-inclusive bundle on that same standard, or a luxury / ship-within-a-ship product.

A departure can appear more than once. The same sailing is listed once per website that sells it, and once per rate tier it still qualifies under. Use Source to tell an agency quote from the line’s own site, and Rate (or the cards below) to pick a fare type.

Prices stay in the currency that website published. Nothing is converted, so a dollar figure is not comparable to a euro one until you filter by Currency. Per night is the fare divided by the nights the sailing spans — not the marketed day count.

What the rates mean

Each row is one qualifying fare for one departure. Click a card to show only that tier.

Economy

Cabin and meals; extras on board

The cruise fare for a balcony cabin or better, without a drinks or Wi-Fi package. Main dining is included; gratuities, drinks, and excursions are usually paid separately. Celebrity’s own search only publishes this kind of lead-in price, so its rows are always Economy. A sailing is listed here when that fare is under 175 EUR / 200 USD per night.

All included

Drinks and Wi-Fi bundled in

A fare the agency marks as all-inclusive — typically a drinks package and Wi-Fi on top of the same cabin standard. Contitrans uses ALLINC/N1K tariff codes; Crusit spells the fare out as All Inclusive. Holland America’s bundled fares land here too. Listed when the bundle is under 300 EUR / 345 USD per night.

Ultra

Luxury all-inclusive, or a ship-within-a-ship

The whole ship on Seabourn, Silversea, Regent, or Viking; or a premium enclave such as Celebrity The Retreat, NCL The Haven, or MSC Yacht Club. MSC and NCL only appear in this tier — their cheaper cabins are ignored. Listed under 350 EUR / 405 USD per night.

  • Prices are per person, in the currency that website published. Nothing is converted, so a dollar figure is not comparable to a euro one until you filter by currency.
  • Per night is the fare divided by the nights the sailing spans (not the marketed day count).
  • Interior cabins are never listed. An ordinary ocean-view stateroom is dropped too, but an ocean-view suite on an all-suite line is kept.
